# Tryggsignal PBL Domain Guard

Use for building permit, notification, preliminary decisions, demolition/land permits, PBL supervision, OVK, technical consultation, start/final clearance, referrals and hearings.

Never encode changing law or national classifications as permanent model knowledge. Every legal/deterministic rule must have source, version, effective_from/effective_until and evidence. Verify current authoritative guidance before changing legal workflow logic.

AI may classify, extract, summarize, compare and draft; it must not become the legal rule engine or final decision maker. Store human review and exact rule/source evidence for consequential findings. Existing cases retain the rule/workflow/classification versions they started under unless an explicit migration policy says otherwise.
